# Evidence compiler — Palestine and Israel research ledger

This public repository develops a source-attributed historical research ledger covering reported Israeli military, state, settler, and related actions from 1948 onward. The incident register is a research compilation; inclusion does not itself establish a legal finding or adjudicate disputed claims.

## Research method and current scope

The current draft literature matrix and incident scan extend through 1972. Annual records distinguish United Nations findings and observations, party complaints, Israeli rationales and denials, foreign press, witness testimony, and unresolved evidentiary limits. Displacement estimates remain separately attributed and are not combined when populations or event counts may overlap. U.S. casualties and incidents are included only where the reviewed sources support the attribution; cases without evidence of Israeli state involvement are explicitly excluded from that category.

Each year is an initial scan. Casualty and displacement totals, detention records, contemporary non-Israeli press, and source-specific records require continued reconstruction and independent review. A report, allegation, or UN condemnation must not be restated as a court judgment unless a judicial source supports that description.
