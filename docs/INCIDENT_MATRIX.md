# Incident Literature Matrix

**Opening case: King David Hotel, Jerusalem, 22 July 1946.** This matrix is organized around documented harm to U.S. citizens and U.S. forces attributed to Israeli actors, plus the formation-era case that opens the history. The King David Hotel incident is an explicit exception category: the actor was a pre-state armed organization, not the Israeli state; it anchors the historical record but is not counted as an Israeli state-action case.

**Cutoff:** 25 November 2026. **Status:** working literature matrix; entries are source-linked leads, not adjudicated findings. See [method and evidence grades](SOURCES_AND_METHOD.md). The [CSV register](../data/incidents.csv) is the normalized index.

**Core King David sources**

1. U.S. Consul in Jerusalem to Secretary of State, 22 July 1946, *Foreign Relations of the United States*: [original U.S. diplomatic document](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1946v07/d508). Grade A. It says Americans in the hotel and vicinity escaped injury at the time of the telegram.
2. British House of Commons, 31 July 1946: [Hansard casualty statement](https://hansard.parliament.uk/Commons/1946-07-31/debates/82d4decf-0ab4-49a3-bc1a-27dcbe822471/Palestine). Grade A for the then-current official count (84 dead, 46 injured, 22 missing), not final count.
3. Bruce Hoffman, “The bombing of the King David Hotel, July 1946,” *Small Wars & Insurgencies* 31:3 (2020), 594–611: [St Andrews research repository record](https://research-portal.st-andrews.ac.uk/en/publications/the-bombing-of-the-king-david-hotel-july-1946/). Grade B; explicitly characterizes Irgun as a Jewish terrorist organization and analyzes military-target and warning questions.
4. UK National Army Museum, [The British Army in Palestine](https://www.nam.ac.uk/explore/british-army-palestine). Grade B; describes Irgun and the attack, including civilians among the dead.
5. Jewish Telegraphic Agency, contemporaneous casualty report, 24 July 1946: [initial list of Jewish casualties](https://www.jta.org/archive/20-jews-listed-among-first-casualties-of-jerusalem-bombing-eight-known-dead). Grade C for contemporaneous names/updates.
6. Archive catalogue for Edward (Ezra) Sperling: [collection description](https://bo-kedem.s3.eu-central-1.amazonaws.com/files/PDF/catalog-CA-31.pdf). Grade C/D for identity and biography; citizenship remains unresolved. Later narrative account calling him “an American Jew”: [Thurston Clarke, *By Blood and Fire* excerpt](https://www.rahs-open-lid.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Clarke-Thurston-By-Blood-and-Fire_-July-22-1946The-Attack-on-Jerusalem_s-King-David-Hotel-Open-Road-Distribution-2016.pdf).

## E. Literature synthesis and open questions

### Patterns the source base can test

- **Attribution versus accountability:** multiple cases have U.S. or UN assessments identifying Israeli fire or Israeli operations, while criminal investigation status remains unclear or disciplinary steps are reported without prosecution.
- **Nationality verification:** dual citizenship and community ties are often reported inconsistently; do not build totals from one legislative statement or advocacy tracker.
- **Evidence access:** families and witnesses may hold key records, while Israeli military/police files and U.S. diplomatic records may be unavailable or delayed.
- **Distinct harm categories:** lethal force, injury at protest, detention/ill-treatment, strike casualties, and harm to serving U.S. personnel require different legal and evidentiary tests.
- **Palestinian-centered reporting:** center Palestinian-American families and witnesses, Palestinian local records, and the occupation/settlement context; corroborate claims using primary records without treating institutional status as a substitute for evidentiary review.

### Highest-priority next work

1. Verify the U.S. citizenship claim for Ezra/Edward Sperling from archival records; do not infer citizenship from “American Jew” or migration history.
2. Obtain the original casualty lists and police inquiry for the King David bombing; reconcile the official 84/46/22 snapshot with later 91-death total.
3. Add person-level citations and source snapshots for each U.S. citizen, especially lower-confidence 2014, 2016, 2025 and 2026 cases.
4. Track current investigation outcomes in every case and ask what the U.S. government did, what Israel disclosed, and what families were told.
5. Search U.S. State Department historical records, U.S. Navy/Marine archives, court records, Palestinian NGOs and local reporting for other in-scope cases.
6. Keep actors outside the inclusion rule in an exclusions register; do not allow the presence of U.S. victims to silently broaden attribution scope.
